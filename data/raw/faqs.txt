Q: Why was my scholarship application rejected?
A: Common reasons for scholarship rejections include:
1. Income certificate expired or issued by unauthorized officer (must be issued by Revenue Officer / Tehsildar).
2. Name mismatch between Aadhaar card, bank passbook, and educational marksheet.
3. Bank account not seeded with Aadhaar (Direct Benefit Transfer - DBT failure).
4. Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) lacking digital barcode/verification number.
5. Incomplete biometric e-KYC on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).
Solution: Re-verify all names across documents, ensure bank account has active NPCI Aadhaar mapping, and upload updated certificates before institutional deadline.

Q: How do I correct spelling errors in my Aadhaar Card?
A:
1. Visit myAadhaar online portal (myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) or your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra.
2. Submit a valid Proof of Identity (POI) document containing the correct spelling (such as PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID, or SSLC Marksheet).
3. If changing full name due to marriage or legal name change, submit Gazette Notification or registered Marriage Certificate.
4. Pay standard fee of ₹50 for demographic update.
5. Track status using Update Request Number (URN) provided on the acknowledgment slip.

Q: What is the procedure to file a Right to Information (RTI) query?
A:
1. Identify the public authority (Ministry/Department) holding the required information.
2. Formulate concise, direct questions asking for specific records, documents, or status logs. Avoid seeking opinions or speculative advice.
3. Visit rtionline.gov.in for Central Government departments or State RTI portals for state departments.
4. Pay the application fee of ₹10 via UPI, Netbanking, or Postal Order (IPO). BPL cardholders are exempt from payment.
5. Under Section 7(1) of RTI Act 2005, the Public Information Officer (PIO) must respond within 30 days.
6. If response is delayed or unsatisfactory, file First Appeal within 30 days to First Appellate Authority.

Q: How can I obtain an Income Certificate in my state?
A:
1. Apply online via your State e-District portal or visit nearest Common Service Center (CSC) / Meeseva / E-Seva.
2. Documents required: Salary slip / Form 16 / Income Affidavit, Bank Statement (last 6 months), Aadhaar Card, Address Proof, and self-declaration of family income.
3. Village Revenue Officer (VRO) or Revenue Inspector conducts field inquiry to verify income claims.
4. Certificate is digitally signed by Tehsildar / Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) and issued within 7 to 15 working days.
5. Always check certificate validity (usually 1 financial year).

Q: How do I resolve Aadhaar-Bank Account NPCI seeding issues for DBT?
A:
1. Visit your bank branch where your account is held.
2. Submit "Aadhaar Seeding / NPCI Mapping Consent Form" along with self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card.
3. Request bank officer to map your account with NPCI mapper for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
4. Verify status online on myAadhaar portal under "Bank Seeding Status".

Q: What are the eligibility rules for Economically Weaker Section (EWS) reservation?
A:
1. Family gross annual income must be below ₹8.0 Lakh from all sources (salary, agriculture, business, profession) for the financial year prior to application.
2. Exclusion criteria: Families holding 5 acres of agricultural land or above, residential flat of 1000 sq ft or above, or residential plot of 100 sq yards or above in notified municipalities.
3. Certificate issuing authority: District Magistrate, Additional District Magistrate, Revenue Officer not below rank of Tehsildar.
